    04

    Started with customers. Moved to product.

    I started in customer-facing lending support, where problems show up first: a rejected application, a stuck mandate, an unclear EMI.

    Over time I cared less about closing the ticket and more about why it existed, and what Product, Engineering and AI could do about it.

    That took me from customer conversations into funnel improvement, in-app guidance, automation and AI, each tied to a number I could track.

    Now I want to own the products that fix the underlying problem, not just the symptoms.

    How I work

    Cross-functional by habit: Product, Engineering and AI teams. I start from customer evidence, pick the smallest change that can move a metric, and report the result with its context.

    My customer-facing years are the advantage: they show me exactly where lending products lose people.
